# Admin guide

This is for whoever adds projects to the website. You do not need to know anything about websites. If you can post photos from your phone, you can do this.

The admin console lives at **your-site.com/admin**. Save it to your phone's home screen or bookmark it.

The console works in English and Spanish. Tap **Español** (bottom left on a computer, top right on a phone) to switch. Hay una versión corta en español al final de esta guía.

## Logging in

1. Open `/admin`.
2. Type the admin email (`chaidezjason@gmail.com`) and the password. Tap **Log in**.
3. Forgot the password? Tap **Forgot your password?**, type the email, and open the link that arrives in the inbox. It lets you set a new one.

If you see "Your session ended", just log in again. Only the registered admin account can get in; any other account is turned away.

## Adding a project, start to finish

1. On **Projects**, tap **New project**.
2. Type the project name, for example `Two-Flat Masonry Restoration`. Tap **Create project**.
3. The project opens as a **draft**. Fill in the top box:
   - **Year**: pick it from the list.
   - **Project value**: optional. Type plain numbers, `185000`. The site shows it as `$185,000`. Leave it empty and nothing shows.
   - **Location**: one line, for example `South Chicago, IL` or `Evergreen Park, IL`.
   - **Description**: what was done and where, in your own words. Leave an empty line between paragraphs. The first sentences become the short preview in the lists automatically.
4. **Select photos**: tap it and pick every photo for the project at once from your camera roll, 4 to 15 is typical. Photos come straight from the phone, HEIC included. Wait for "All photos uploaded."
5. The first photo is the cover. Tap **Set as cover** under another one to change that. Use the arrows (or drag on a computer) to change the order.
6. Tap **Publish**. Done. **View on site** opens the live page.

That is the whole job. Nothing else on the screen is required.

## What the rest of the screen is for

- **Optional details**: the web address (made from the name, leave it alone), a scope of work list (one item per line, shown on the project page), and a switch to feature the project on the home page. When no project is featured, the home page simply shows the newest ones.
- **Spanish translation**: a Spanish name, description and location. Leave any of them empty and Spanish readers see the English text. You can come back to this any time.
- **Optional photo details** under each photo: a description for screen readers and search engines (the project name is used when it is empty), a caption, and a "before" or "after" tag that builds a before and after pair.
- **Save** or **Save draft** keeps changes without publishing. **Unpublish** hides a published project without deleting it.

## Editing a project

Open **Projects**, tap the project name, change what you want, tap **Save**.

## Hiding a project

Open the project and tap **Unpublish**. It stays in the console but disappears from the site. Tap **Publish** to bring it back.

## Deleting a project

Open the project, scroll to the bottom, tap **Delete project** and confirm. This removes the project and all of its photos for good. If you are not sure, unpublish instead.

## Copying a project

**Duplicate** makes a private copy with the same photos and text. Useful when two jobs were similar.

## Changing the order of projects

The site shows the newest year first. Inside a year, the arrows on the **Projects** screen set the order.

## Fixing a mistake

- Wrong photo: **Remove** it and upload the right one.
- Wrong text: change it and **Save**.
- Published too early: **Unpublish**.
- Deleted by accident: deleted projects cannot be restored. Ask the person who set up the site; a database backup may exist.

## Removing the sample projects

The site ships with six sample projects marked "Sample". When your real projects are in, tap **Remove all sample projects** on the Projects screen. This only touches the samples.

## Settings

Under **Settings** you can change the company name, phone, email, location, the home page headline and intro, the About page text, the "What we do" list, the contact page text, the description search engines show, social links, the photo used at the top of the home page and on the About page, and the logo. Tap **Save settings** at the bottom.

Keep every claim true. Do not add licenses, years in business or awards unless they are real.

## Messages

People who use the contact form appear under **Messages** with their phone or email. They also arrive at the company inbox (luisjacinto1107@gmail.com) when email delivery is set up. Tap the phone or email to call or write back.

## Logging out

Tap **Log out** at the bottom left (computer) or top right (phone). Do this on shared computers.

## Guía rápida en español

- Entre en `/admin` con el correo del administrador y la contraseña. ¿Olvidó la contraseña? Toque **¿Olvidó su contraseña?** y siga el enlace del correo.
- **Nuevo proyecto**: escriba el nombre y toque **Crear proyecto**.
- Llene **Año**, **Ubicación** y **Descripción**. El **Valor del proyecto** es opcional: escriba solo números, `185000`.
- **Seleccionar fotos**: elija todas las fotos del proyecto de una vez. Espere a que diga "Todas las fotos se subieron". La primera foto es la portada; toque **Usar como portada** para cambiarla. Ordene con las flechas.
- **Publicar** pone el proyecto en el sitio. **Ocultar** lo esconde. **Guardar** guarda cambios.
- Todo lo demás es opcional: **Detalles opcionales**, **Traducción al español** y los **Detalles opcionales de la foto**. Lo que quede vacío en español se muestra en inglés.
- Cuando ya tenga proyectos reales, toque **Quitar todos los proyectos de muestra**.
- En **Ajustes** cambie teléfono, textos y fotos principales. Toque **Guardar ajustes**.
- Los mensajes del formulario de contacto están en **Mensajes** y también llegan al correo de la empresa.
- **Cerrar sesión** al terminar.
